========================================
         ZEBOMBER - MISSION BRIEFING
========================================

Defend the base from waves of attacking enemies!

CONTROLS:
  W / S / A / D  -  Control the airplane
  Left Mouse     -  Fire rockets
  Space          -  Drop bombs
  Right Mouse    -  Hold to look around (free-look)
  Mouse Scroll   -  Zoom radar in/out
  /              -  Toggle jet HUD on/off
  ESC            -  Pause game

TIPS:
  - Only bombs destroy tanks!
  - Rockets can destroy helicopters
  - Check bottom-right of screen for volume, sensitivity, and HUD controls
  - If the base HP reaches zero, you lose the game!
  - Destroy all enemies in a wave to advance to the next one

Press SPACE to start the game.
